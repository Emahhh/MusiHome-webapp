Privacy, in plain language.

Updated October 22, 2026

MusiHome is developed by Emanuele Buonaccorsi. The current native app has no MusiHome account, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Your music library isn’t uploaded to a MusiHome server.

Your library and layouts

With your permission, MusiHome reads your iPhone’s Music library to display artists, albums, songs, playlists, artwork, and listening-related library information used by its music widgets. It uses Apple’s system music player for playback.

Your layouts, selected artwork, custom photos, wallpaper, preferences, and local diagnostics are stored on your device. Selected music metadata and artwork are shared with MusiHome’s widget extension through its App Group so your widgets can work. The current configuration does not sync your wall through a MusiHome cloud service.

Photos and artwork services

When you choose a photo, MusiHome saves a local copy for the customization you requested. The app also requests music metadata and artwork from Apple services and can search Wikimedia Commons for artist photos. A search can send the search term, such as an artist name, to the relevant service. Network services also receive the information needed to respond to a request, such as an IP address.

These services process requests under their own policies: Apple’s privacy policy and Wikimedia’s privacy policy. Attribution and license information are retained with selected Commons photos.

Purchases

Apple processes in-app payments. MusiHome uses StoreKit to verify ownership and handle purchases, restoration, and revoked transactions. Local purchase diagnostics may include product identifiers, transaction identifiers, price, currency, date, and purchase status. MusiHome does not receive your full card details.

Diagnostics and support

The app keeps local diagnostic records for troubleshooting music, widgets, and purchases. Widget diagnostics can include selected item identifiers and widget-link URLs. Diagnostics are not automatically sent to a MusiHome analytics server. You choose whether to share a report.

If you email the developer, the email address, message, attachments, and any diagnostics you include are processed to respond to your request. Do not include passwords or payment details. Contact emanuele.click.dev@gmail.com to ask about or request deletion of support information you supplied.

This website and legacy bookmarks

This website has no advertising or analytics scripts and no email signup form. The hosting provider necessarily processes requests to serve its pages and assets.

Legacy bookmark links store the destination, title, and artwork URL in the link’s query parameters. The bookmark page uses these values to show installation instructions and open your chosen destination. Its service worker supports the existing bookmark flow. A supplied artwork URL may cause your browser to request an image from that third-party host. Avoid placing private information in a bookmark URL; URLs may appear in browser history and hosting logs.

Your choices and retention

You can revoke music or photo access in iPhone Settings. You can remove items and customizations in the app. Deleting the app removes its local app data, subject to iOS backup and shared-container behavior; Home Screen bookmarks can be removed separately. Apple manages App Store purchase history independently.

Local app data remains until it is replaced, removed, or deleted with the app. Diagnostic histories are bounded by the app. The developer uses support correspondence to handle your request; you can request deletion by email. Third-party service retention is governed by their policies.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated when MusiHome’s data handling changes. For privacy questions, contact Emanuele Buonaccorsi at emanuele.click.dev@gmail.com.
