MusiHome is an independently developed music organizer for iPhone, created by Emanuele Buonaccorsi.

Using MusiHome

MusiHome helps you browse, organize, and play music from your iPhone’s Music library. You are responsible for the content you choose and for using it in accordance with the applicable rights and service terms. The app does not supply music or grant rights to third-party recordings, artwork, or artist images.

Apple Music and other services

Apple Music subscriptions and streaming services are separate from MusiHome. Access to subscription content requires the relevant subscription, regional availability, and an internet connection where applicable. The native MusiHome app does not connect to Spotify. Legacy web bookmarks open the third-party destination originally chosen by the user.

MusiHome is not affiliated with Apple Inc. Apple Music, iPhone, and App Store are trademarks of Apple Inc. Other names, recordings, and artwork belong to their respective owners.

In-app purchases

Some personalization features are available through an in-app purchase. The app displays the localized price supplied by Apple before you purchase. An Apple Music subscription is not included.

Apple handles payment, purchase history, and refund requests. Restore Purchase can recover a verified eligible purchase associated with your Apple account. App Store purchases are subject to Apple’s applicable terms and standard end-user license agreement.

Saved data

The current app configuration stores your wall on your device. Keep suitable device backups. Removing the app can remove its local data. Contact support before deleting the app if a saved wall will not open.

Availability and changes

The app relies on iOS and third-party music and artwork services. Features can be affected by permissions, device compatibility, service availability, and changes made by those providers. The website’s previews show example collections and customization options.
