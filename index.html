Let’s help you out ↗

What is MusiHome?+

A personal music wall for iPhone. It brings albums, artists, songs, and playlists from your Music library into one visual space, with pages, widgets, and your own layout.

Do I need Apple Music?+

You need to allow access to your iPhone’s Music library. An Apple Music subscription is needed for subscription streaming content; it’s separate from MusiHome. MusiHome doesn’t provide music or connect to Spotify.

Can I personalize my collection?+

Choose your layout, change the artwork, create folders, and add photo tiles. Bring your collection together with wallpapers and an app icon that suit your style.

Do the widgets start playing music?+

Tapping a Home Screen widget opens the selected music’s details in MusiHome. It doesn’t start or change playback. Open the details, then choose what to play.

Do I need another account?+

No MusiHome account is required. Your wall is saved on your device. The current app has no ads or tracking SDKs. Read our privacy policy for details about music access, artwork services, and optional diagnostics.

When can I get it?+

MusiHome is getting ready for its iPhone release. This site will link directly to its App Store listing when it’s available. Until then, explore the real app previews here.
