Getting started

MusiHome uses your iPhone’s Music library. Open the app and allow music access, find something you love, then pin it to your wall. You can arrange your collection across pages and add in-app music widgets.

I can’t see my music.+

Check music access in iPhone Settings → Apps → MusiHome. If access is restricted, check Screen Time restrictions. Open Apple’s Music app to confirm that the albums, songs, or playlists are in your library, then return to MusiHome. Music in another service, such as Spotify, won’t appear in the native app.

A song won’t play.+

Try the same song in Apple’s Music app. Subscription streaming requires an active Apple Music subscription and a connection; downloaded library music may be available offline. MusiHome controls the system Music player, so its active queue is shared with Apple Music.

How do I set up a Home Screen widget?+

Touch and hold an empty area of your iPhone Home Screen, choose Edit → Add Widget, and find MusiHome. Add Favorite or Favorites. Then touch and hold that widget, choose Edit Widget, and select its music. Configure each widget separately. Tapping an item opens its details; playback starts only when you choose to play.

My widget opens the wrong item or looks empty.+

Open MusiHome and confirm music access is allowed. Touch and hold the widget, choose Edit Widget, and select its music again. If the item was removed from your library, choose another. If the problem continues, remove and re-add that widget.

How do I restore a purchase?+

Use the Apple account that originally made the purchase. In MusiHome, open Settings → the purchase screen and choose Restore Purchase. Eligible purchases restore their personalization features. If a purchase is pending approval, wait for Apple’s approval or try again from the purchase screen.

My wall couldn’t open. What should I do?+

Use Try Again on the recovery screen. Check that your iPhone has free storage, and restart it if needed. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting: layouts and custom photos are saved locally. Use Share Error Details to send the error to the developer if the problem continues.

Do my layouts sync between devices?+

The current configuration saves layouts and custom photos on your device. Don’t rely on cross-device sync. Your device’s backup settings determine whether local app data is included in a backup.

What about my existing music bookmarks?+

The existing web bookmark system is still supported. Previously created /bookmark/ links keep their music destination, name, and artwork parameters. In Safari, use Share → Add to Home Screen to install a bookmark. Its destination opens when you launch it from your Home Screen. These legacy web links are separate from the native MusiHome app and its widgets.

Purchases and refunds

Apple handles App Store payments and refunds. To request a refund, visit Apple’s Report a Problem page. Contact the developer for help with unlocking features after a verified purchase.
